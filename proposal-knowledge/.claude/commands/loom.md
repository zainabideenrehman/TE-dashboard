description: Loom recording bullets (cues, not a script)

Output bullet cues for Hassan to glance at WHILE recording. He screen-shares
the visual asset. Target 90-150 seconds.

### Loom recording bullets — [job title]

**Open (10-15 sec)**
- Greet by [name or "there"]
- One sentence on what you understood about their problem

**The diagnosis (20-30 sec)**
- Surface problem they posted
- Real problem underneath (the twist)
- Why it matters to their business

**Show the visual (30-45 sec) — screen share now**
- Transition line: [pick the matching one below]
- Walk through: [2-3 things to point at]
- Tie back to their pain

**How you'd approach it (20-30 sec)**
- First milestone you'd ship
- One technical choice + the why
- Closest portfolio project + one-sentence parallel

**Close (10-15 sec)**
- "If this looks like what you need, hit reply and we can set up a quick call"
- Drop name once more if known

**Pre-record checklist**
- Visual asset open in browser tab
- Webcam on, framed
- Mic check
- Close Slack, email, anything that pings

## Transition lines (insert the matching one)
AGENT FLOW: "let me show you how the agent would actually think through this.
here's the flow." / "quick look at how this would work end to end." / "here's
the loop the agent runs. clean handoffs between the model and your tools."
BEFORE/AFTER: "here's what your process looks like today on the left, and what
it'd look like after on the right." / "manual version. automated version. same
outcome, way less effort." / "red steps are the ones eating your time."
HTML MOCKUP: "I roughed out what your dashboard could look like. placeholder
data, but this is the shape of it." / "here's a quick mockup with your
branding." / "this is rough but it gives you a feel for the layout."
ARCHITECTURE: "here's how the pieces fit together. data sources on the left,
middleware in the middle, what your team sees on the right." / "quick
architecture view. nothing locked in, but this is how I'd wire it on day one."
ERD: "here's the data model I'd start with. core tables and how they relate."
/ "quick look at the schema. clean relationships, easy to extend."
SPRINT TIMELINE: "here's how I'd phase the build. week one is foundation. week
two is where you see real progress." / "by end of week two you'd have something
to click through."
WIREFRAMES: "here are the three main screens. login, home feed, detail view."
/ "tap here, lands here, takes this action. clean path."
STRATEGY COMPARISON: "since the brief is open, I sketched two ways to go.
option A is faster. option B is more flexible." / "tradeoffs on both sides."

Transition rules: pause half a beat after the line. Never say "as you can see"
or "obviously". Transition out with "alright, back to me" or cut to the close.
