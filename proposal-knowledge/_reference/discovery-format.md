# Discovery Briefing Format

Write this to `briefing.md` in the current proposal folder. Use these exact
sections.

### In plain English
A beginner-friendly walkthrough of the whole situation, placed first so it
orients the reader before the detail. Assume the reader knows nothing about the
client, the product, or the tech. Short sentences, simple words, no jargon (or
explain it the moment you use it). Cover the whole picture in order: who the
client is, what they already have, what they actually want and why, where the
catch is, and what winning the job would take. This is the "explain it to a
smart friend who is new to all this" version of the entire briefing.

### Client profile
- Name (if visible in feedback or post)
- Company / business (what they do, infer from search + post)
- Industry / vertical (flag if it matches a common vertical)
- Size signals (solo founder, small team, mid-market, enterprise)
- Upwork history signals if relevant (hire rate, total spent, jobs posted,
  pattern of past hires)

### What they're asking for
- Surface-level deliverable (what the post literally says)
- Explicit must-haves (bullets)
- Nice-to-haves (bullets)
- Scope boundaries (in / out / ambiguous)
- Screening questions (list verbatim if present, so /questions is available)

### Glossary
Plain-language explanation of every jargon term, tool, acronym, framework,
business term, or regulatory reference (HIPAA, COPPA, SOC2, etc).
Format: `- TermName: one-sentence plain explanation. Why it matters here.`

### Pain point hypothesis
The REAL problem under the surface request. What's costing them money, time, or
trust? What have they probably already tried that failed? Why pay someone now?
2-4 sentences.

### Red flags and risks
- Anything off (budget too low for scope, unclear ownership, vague brief,
  hostile tone, scope creep, "just one small thing" language)
- Questions Hassan should ask before bidding
- Tire-kicker / bad-fit signals

### Tech stack notes
- What they're using or implying
- Gaps to flag (voice AI but no telephony named, RAG but no vector store, etc.)
- Stack decisions Hassan would need to make
- Default to Hassan's stack unless the job calls for else. Flag deviations.

### Strategic angle
One paragraph: how to position, which portfolio project to anchor on, what the
hook + twist might look like. (Read `_reference/portfolio/_index.md` to pick the
anchor.)

---
End with: "What do you want to dig into?"
