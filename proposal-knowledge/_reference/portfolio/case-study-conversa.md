# Conversa

**Product:** Tech Emulsion's voice AI agent platform
**URL:** conversa.ai
**Type:** Productized SaaS

## The problem

Shopify stores leave money on the table every time a customer abandons their cart. Email sequences get ignored. SMS gets blocked. Most stores never recover the sale.

Bigger brands need voice AI too, but generic call platforms can't access store data, follow up on real orders, or stay compliant with do-not-call rules.

## What we built

Conversa powers AI voice agents for two segments.

**For Shopify stores.** Outbound AI calls recover abandoned carts. The agent knows what's in the cart, offers a live discount code, and texts the checkout link during the call. Every call records transcripts and sentiment. Klaviyo gets the data.

**For enterprise.** Custom voice agents tied to internal systems. Checkout Champ is one integration. Others are bespoke.

## Pricing

- Shopify free tier: $0.75 per call
- Shopify Pro: $499 per month at $0.35 per call

## Stack

Retell AI for the voice engine. Node and Express for business logic. n8n for orchestration. PostgreSQL on Railway, refactored multi-tenant. React admin dashboard.

## Result

Conversa runs as a real product on the Shopify App Store. Listed under AI Efficiencies LLC, San Diego. The Meatery is the flagship deployment and case study.
