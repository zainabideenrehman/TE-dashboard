# BillboardIQ

**Industry:** Out-of-home (OOH) advertising management
**Type:** Lovable prototype takeover, rebuilt for production
**URL:** ooh.express (live brand: OOH Express)
**Lovable:** Rescue — Lovable prototype rebuilt to production off-platform.  [lovable:rescue]

## The problem

OOH advertising teams juggle billboards, sites, campaigns, and clients across cities. The data is geographic by nature: where is each board, who's on it, when does the campaign end. Most teams manage this in spreadsheets, with no map view and no real reporting.

The client had a Lovable prototype that hinted at the right product but couldn't run in production. UI was there. The backend, performance, and structure weren't.

## What we built

A production-grade OOH ad management SaaS rebuilt from the Lovable prototype.

- **Geo-visualization of inventory.** Boards plotted on a map with status, campaign, and metadata at a glance.
- **Campaign and client management.** Bookings, end dates, occupancy, all tied to real records.
- **Reporting dashboards.** Recharts-based visualizations for campaign performance and inventory utilization.
- **Production backend.** Migrated off Lovable's bundled stack to a Supabase-backed setup that holds up under real usage.

## Stack

Frontend: React. Backend: Supabase. Charts: Recharts.

## Result

What was a Lovable demo became a working SaaS the client can operate against real inventory and real campaigns. The Lovable-to-production playbook from this project informs how Tech Emulsion now positions Prototype Takeover engagements.
