# Podcast Beacon

**Industry:** Podcasting, link-in-bio
**Type:** SaaS

## The problem

Podcasters live in fragments. Episodes on Apple, Spotify, YouTube. Newsletter on Substack. Merch on Shopify. Social posts on five platforms. Listeners hit one of these and can't find the rest.

Link-in-bio tools like Linktree work for influencers but don't speak the language of podcasting. No episode previews. No "listen on your favorite app" routing. No way to surface latest episode and merch in the same view.

## What we built

A link-in-bio SaaS purpose-built for podcasters.

- **Episode-aware hub.** Latest episode, archive, "listen on your platform" links all on one page.
- **Multi-platform routing.** One link routes the listener to their default podcast app.
- **Branded pages.** Hosts customize the look to match their show.
- **Subscriptions and merch.** Sell directly from the hub through Stripe.

## Stack

MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node). Stripe for payments. AWS for hosting and storage.

## Result

A working link-in-bio product designed around the podcaster's workflow, not the generic creator's.
