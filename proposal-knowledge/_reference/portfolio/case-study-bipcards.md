# Bipcards

**Client:** Bipcards.com
**Industry:** Customer reviews and engagement, local business
**Type:** SaaS with physical NFC card fulfillment

## The problem

Local businesses know reviews drive new customers. Getting them is the hard part. Asking customers face-to-face feels awkward. Email follow-ups get ignored. Most customers leave the shop and forget.

The fastest path is putting a review prompt directly in the customer's hand at the moment of payment. NFC cards and QR codes can do this, but a card by itself isn't a product. Businesses need a way to program the cards, change destinations, track results, and order more.

## What we built

A SaaS that pairs programmable NFC cards and QR codes with a full management platform.

- **Programmable cards.** Businesses program their NFC cards and QR codes to point at any destination. Google Reviews. Yelp. A booking page. Anywhere.

- **Real-time analytics.** Track taps, scans, and conversions. See which locations and which staff drive the most engagement.

- **Subscription billing.** Flexible plans that match how the business uses the product. Stripe-backed.

- **Sales rep workflow.** Reps onboard new businesses and program cards on the spot, which turns a sales call into a working setup before they leave.

- **Card shop.** Order new branded NFC cards directly through the platform.

## Stack

MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node). Next.js for the frontend. Stripe for subscriptions.

## Result

Bipcards turned a commodity NFC card into a recurring SaaS product. Local businesses get a tap-to-review system that actually works. Sales reps close and provision in the same visit. The platform handles billing, analytics, and reordering on autopilot.
