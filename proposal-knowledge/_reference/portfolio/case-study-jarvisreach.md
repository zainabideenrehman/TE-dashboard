# JarvisReach

**Industry:** Sales technology, B2B lead generation
**Type:** LinkedIn prospecting and email outreach SaaS

## The problem

B2B sales teams stitch their pipeline together from five tools. One scrapes LinkedIn. Another finds emails. A third scores leads. A fourth runs sequences. The data fragments. The work duplicates. Reps spend hours moving prospects between systems instead of selling.

Even with the right stack, most teams don't have a clean view of what's working. Which message converts. Which list outperforms. Which rep is keeping the pipeline alive.

## What we built

A single SaaS that runs the entire prospecting and outreach motion from one place.

- **ICP-based lead discovery.** Users define their ideal customer profile by industry, role, company size, and location. The platform pulls matching leads from LinkedIn automatically.

- **Contact enrichment.** Verified email addresses and company data attached to every lead. No more manual lookups.

- **Lead scoring.** Each prospect gets a fit score based on ICP criteria, so reps focus on the highest-probability conversations first.

- **Multi-step sequenced outreach.** Email sequences with branching logic. Reply detection. Stop conditions.

- **Team leaderboards.** Reps see their own activity and how they stack against the team. Healthy competition baked in.

- **Admin analytics.** Owners see what's working at the campaign, sequence, and rep level.

- **Subscription flexibility.** Plans tied to usage so teams can scale up or down without contract pain.

## Stack

Next.js, FastAPI, Supabase, Python.

## Result

JarvisReach replaces the typical 4-to-5-tool stack with one platform. Sales managers stop chasing data across systems. Reps stop copy-pasting. The team gets one dashboard that shows what's actually converting.
